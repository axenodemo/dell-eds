Privacy notice — JSW Motors launch updates

This notice explains how JSW Motors ("we") handles the personal data you share through the registration form on this page, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What we collect

Your full name, mobile number, email address (if you choose to provide it), your consent record, and the campaign reference that brought you to this page.

Why we collect it

Solely to send you updates about the upcoming JSW Motors launch and related announcements. We do not use this data for any other purpose, and we do not sell or share it with third parties for their marketing.

Consent and withdrawal

We process your data only with your explicit consent, recorded at the time of submission. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us at privacy@jswmotors.in, after which we will stop contacting you and erase your data.

Storage and retention

Your data is stored securely on servers located in India (Mumbai region) and retained only as long as needed for launch communications, or until you withdraw consent.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to access, correct, and erase your personal data, and to grievance redressal. Contact privacy@jswmotors.in for any request.
